# Tiramisu - straight from Italy

## Reference

Friend with a rich Italian heritage

## Ingredients

Enough for a 9 x 13 pan and some extra

| Item                        | Measurement                  | Weight | Notes                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ladyfingers                 | 1 pack                       |        | Bonomi Organic Savoiardi. Get them from Italian supermarkets - they move fast so they're fresh |
| Espresso                    | 9 double shots + 200ml water |        | Blue Bottle Hayes Valley Espresso. Important to get this right - makes a lot of difference     |
| Large egg yolks             | 12                           |        | Straight from the fridge                                                                       |
| Sugar                       |                              | 110g   |                                                                                                |
| All purpose flour           |                              | 15g    |                                                                                                |
| Whipping cream              |                              | 650g   | Straight from the fridge                                                                       |
| Mascarpone cheese           | 3 tubs (226g each)           | 24oz   | Straight from the fridge                                                                       |
| Semi sweet chocolate shaved |                              | 6-8oz  | Nice to have                                                                                   |

## Steps

1. Mix the espresso and water in a shallow dish
2. Dip the ladyfingers in the espresso sugar side first, then flip them thrice quickly so they end up sugar side up, and place them in the pan. Soaking them longer makes them brittle and crumbly
3. Empty the mascarpone straight from the fridge into the mixer bowl and beat it lightly
4. Break the eggs straight from the fridge and separate the yolks - we only need the yolks. Cold eggs are easier to separate
5. Whisk egg yolks, sugar and flour in a heavy sauce pan
6. Keep stirring with a whisk: 1.5 minutes on medium heat, 1 minute on low heat, then take the pan off the heat for the last 30 seconds. Don't let the eggs cook - it should be slightly thick and still runny. This timing assumes the eggs are straight from the fridge
7. Add the egg yolk mixture to the mascarpone and mix until it's mixed completely. No need to add a lot of air - the whipped cream takes care of that
8. Move the mascarpone mixture to another bowl
9. Whip the cream straight from the fridge in the mixer bowl until it has soft peaks
10. Fold one-third of the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture
11. Add this into the remaining whipped cream and fold until smooth - [how to fold](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YoZcebw9XZo)
12. Spread half over the ladyfingers in the pan
13. Assemble the next layer of ladyfingers the same way as step 2
14. Spread the remaining half of the cream over the pan
15. Refrigerate for at least 8 hours - it tastes best on the third day
16. A couple of hours before serving, sift cocoa and sprinkle it evenly. Any earlier and the cocoa pulls moisture out of the cream and turns into a soggy, bitter paste
17. Add thinly shaved chocolate over it (nice to have)
